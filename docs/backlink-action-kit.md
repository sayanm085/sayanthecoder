Do not send the draft until the specific recipient, page fit and live resource are checked. No contact list or reply rate has been invented.

## What to measure

At the first available baseline, record the target page, referring page/domain, actual link/mention, referral enquiries and relevant country/query metrics. At later reviews compare meaningful enquiries and page-level referring domains, with date ranges and source limitations. Use Search Console or a working backlink provider to establish a real link gap before assigning numerical targets.

Avoid bulk directory submission, paid followed links, private blog networks and exact-match-anchor demands. [Google's link-spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam) explains why. Third-party editorial links and rankings cannot be guaranteed.

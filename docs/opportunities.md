## Measurement and timing

- Live production was verified at 23 sitemap URLs before this new pass. This pass builds 26 locally, including laundry, games and privacy. These latest changes still require deployment.
- US mobile-app volume has an extreme recent spike (1,900 → 1,900 → 33,100). Australian MVP CPC is an outlier. Neither drives the recommendations.
- A provider KD of zero is not a Google score or a guarantee of an easy ranking. Query variants can overlap; do not sum volume into a global audience.
- The `.in` domain is a country-domain signal toward India. It does not establish that foreign rankings are impossible; no domain change was made. Google's regional guidance: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites
- Google says search effects can take hours to months and generally recommends weeks for assessment: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide
- Same-day GA4 country-report request was refused because the tool only accepts completed Analytics days. No traffic or customer result was inferred.
